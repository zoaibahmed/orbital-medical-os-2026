: NASA SPACE APP CHALANGE PROJET RECUIREMENT AND GUID LINES
1. 3D Holographic Medical Bay (Frontend & Environment)
Three.js / React Three Fiber Setup: Ek immersive 3D space station medical bay environment render karein jahan user interactive UI ke sath navigate kar sake.
Holographic UI Panels: Floating 3D holographic screens lagayein jo astronauts ke vitals, health status, aur diagnostic data ko futuristic style mein display karein.
2. Crew Biometrics & Vitals Real-Time Tracking Module
Heart Rate & Blood Pressure Monitor: Astronauts ki cardiovascular adaptation aur heart rate variability (HRV) ko track karne ke liye live simulated charts banayein.
Oxygen Saturation ($SpO_2$) & Hypoxia Warning: Spacewalks ke dauran hypoxia (low oxygen) ke risk ko monitor karne ke liye alert system add karein.
Sleep & Circadian Rhythm Tracker: Isolation aur irregular schedules ki wajah se sleep loss ko track karne ke liye daily sleep-score logging module banayein.
3. Space Radiation Monitoring & Risk Dashboard (CANS Integration Inspired)
Radiation Exposure Visualizer: Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR) aur Solar Particle Events (SPE) ke levels ko track karne ke liye ek gauge/dashboard banayein (jaise Canadian Space Agency ka CANS neutron spectrometer data represent karta hai).
Cumulative Dose Calculator: Mission duration ke hisab se astronaut ki cumulative radiation absorption aur cancer/tissue damage risk level calculate karne ki logic likhein.
4. Physiological & Musculoskeletal Risk Assessment
Bone Density & Muscle Loss Tracker: Microgravity ki wajah se hone wale bone breakdown aur muscle atrophy ko simulate karne ke liye exercise log (countermeasure tracker) banayein.
SANS (Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome) Checker: Vision changes aur intracranial pressure symptoms ko self-assess karne ke liye ek interactive questionnaire aur diagnostic checklist banayein.
5. Behavioral & Psychological Health (Isolation & Confinement Countermeasure)
Mental Well-being Check-in: Lambi isolation ke douran behavioral changes aur psychiatric risks ko monitor karne ke liye daily mood/stress level logging slider banayein.
Coping & Support Resource Hub: Astronauts ke liye relaxation guides, communication delay management tips, aur automated AI psychological support prompt integrate karein.
6. Emergency Protocols & AI Diagnostics Assistant
AI Health Diagnostics Tool: Vitals ya radiation levels threshold cross karne par AI-driven medical recommendations generate karein.
Emergency Override Protocol: Critical alarms (jaise sudden depressurization, high radiation spike, ya severe crew injury) ke liye ek 1-click Emergency Protocol screen trigger karein jo immediate emergency steps show kare.
