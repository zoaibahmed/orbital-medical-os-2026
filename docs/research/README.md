# Vitals Orbit — Space Health Research & Alert Guidelines

**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**

**Contributor:** Fatima
**Role:** Space Health Domain Researcher & Content Specialist

## Project Overview

Vitals Orbit is an astronaut health monitoring application designed to support health awareness and decision-making during long-duration space missions.

My contribution focuses on scientific research, health-risk descriptions, alert guidelines, and recommendations based on NASA's Human Research Program and relevant space-health research.

## My Contributions

### 1. Space Health Research
Researched NASA's five hazards of spaceflight:

- Space radiation
- Isolation and confinement
- Distance from Earth
- Altered gravity
- Hostile and closed environments

### 2. Health Monitoring and Alert Guidelines
Prepared research-informed content for monitoring:

- Heart rate and cardiovascular changes
- Sleep disruption and fatigue
- Mood, stress, and behavioral well-being
- Radiation exposure
- Bone and muscle health
- Vision-related symptoms associated with SANS

### 3. Developer Alert Specifications
Created `alert_rules.json` to help the backend developer implement explainable alerts, including severity levels, contributing indicators, and suggested follow-up actions.

The prototype alert thresholds are illustrative and are not NASA-approved clinical thresholds.

Vitals Orbit uses simulated astronaut health data. Its alerts and recommendations are research-informed prototype examples, not validated medical assessments, diagnoses, or treatment instructions.

The application is intended for educational and hackathon demonstration purposes only.
